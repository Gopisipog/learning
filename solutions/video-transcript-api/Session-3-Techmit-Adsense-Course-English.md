## Solving the "One Article at a Time" Problem

**Problem:** Article Forge only generates one article at a time. New articles go into queue.

**Solution:** Use Chrome Profiles (Persons)

### Setup Multiple Chrome Profiles:
1. In Chrome, click your profile icon (top right)
2. Click "Add" or "Guest"
3. Create multiple persons: Member 1, Member 2, etc.
4. Each profile has separate cookies/history (like a fresh browser)

### Workflow:
- Create 3-4 Article Forge accounts (using PayPal method)
- Login to each account in different Chrome profiles
- Generate articles simultaneously in all profiles
- **Result:** 3 articles generating at once instead of 1!

**Scaling Example:**

- 1 website content ready in 10 minutes
- 6 websites content per hour
- 20-50 websites per day possible!

**Important:** Always use incognito for payment/cracking. Use Chrome profiles for content generation.

**Key Point:** The domain must be a top-level domain, NOT a subdomain. We've approved and sold AdSense accounts on various extensions.
