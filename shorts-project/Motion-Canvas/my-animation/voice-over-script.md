# Voice-Over Script for "Benny the Bunny's Adventure"

## Scene 1: Title Screen (5 seconds)
**Narrator:** "Benny the Bunny's Adventure... A Magical Forest Story"

## Scene 2: Bunny Scene (8 seconds)
**Narrator:** "One sunny morning in the magical forest, meet Benny the Bunny! With his fluffy white fur and pink ears, Benny loved to hop and play."

## Scene 3: Bird Scene (7 seconds)
**Narrator:** "Soon, Benny met Bella the Bird, perched high in a tree. Bella flapped her wings and chirped, 'Hello, Benny! Let's go on an adventure!'"

## Scene 4: Finale Scene (10 seconds)
**Narrator:** "Together, they found the magic rainbow! Benny, Bella, and their butterfly friend jumped for joy. And they all lived happily ever after. The End!"

---

## Instructions to Generate Voice-Over:

### Option 1: Use Natural Readers (Free Online TTS)
1. Go to https://www.naturalreaders.com/online/
2. Copy each scene's narration text
3. Select a voice (recommend: Female voice for kids story)
4. Click "Play" to preview
5. Click "Download" to save as MP3
6. Save files as:
   - `scene1-narration.mp3`
   - `scene2-narration.mp3`
   - `scene3-narration.mp3`
   - `scene4-narration.mp3`

### Option 2: Use TTSMaker (Free, No Login Required)
1. Go to https://ttsmaker.com/
2. Paste the narration text
3. Select voice: English (US) - Female
4. Click "Convert to Speech"
5. Download the MP3 file

### Option 3: Use Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (High Quality)
1. Go to https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech
2. Try the demo with your script
3. Download the audio files

### Option 4: Record Your Own Voice
1. Use Windows Voice Recorder or Audacity (free)
2. Read the script in a friendly, animated voice
3. Export as MP3
