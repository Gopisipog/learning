## 🎨 Design Improvements

### Typography
- **Larger Text** - 55-110px (vs 50-90px before)
- **Bolder Fonts** - Weight 700-800 (vs 600-700)
- **Better Shadows** - Larger blur radius for readability
- **Multi-line Format** - Subtitles split across 2 lines

- **Darker Background** - #0a0a1a (deeper black)
- **Gold Accents** - #FFD700 for titles
