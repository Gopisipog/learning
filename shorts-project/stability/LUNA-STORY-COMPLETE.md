## 💡 **Tips & Tricks**

### Adjust Voiceover Volume
Edit `assemble-complete-video.js` line with `volume=1.5`:
- Louder: `volume=2.0`
- Quieter: `volume=1.0`

### Change Music Volume
Edit `assemble-complete-video.js` line with `volume=0.15`:
- Louder: `volume=0.25`
- Quieter: `volume=0.10`

### Change Scene Duration
Edit `create-complete-story.js` and modify `duration: 5` to your preference

### Use Different Voice
Edit `generate-complete-voiceovers.py` and change `VOICE` variable:
- `en-US-AriaNeural` - Warm female
- `en-GB-SoniaNeural` - British female
- `en-US-GuyNeural` - Friendly male
