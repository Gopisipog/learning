## 🎨 Customization Ideas

### Create More Stories
Edit `story-generator.js` to create new stories:
- Different animals (dragons, dinosaurs, unicorns)
- Different themes (space adventure, underwater journey)
- Different seasons (winter wonderland, summer beach)

### Modify Rhymes
Edit `create-rhyme-video.js` to change:
- Rhyme text
- Scene duration (currently 4 seconds each)
- Transition effects

### Change Video Format
In `create-rhyme-video.js`, modify the scale filter:
- **Square (1:1):** `scale=1080:1080` - Instagram posts
- **Horizontal (16:9):** `scale=1920:1080` - YouTube videos
- **Vertical (9:16):** `scale=1080:1920` - Shorts/Reels (current)
